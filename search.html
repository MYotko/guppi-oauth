GUPPI search

Last updated November 12, 2026.

GUPPI is a private household assistant run by its owner on the owner's own computers. Its web search is a private SearXNG instance that answers only that one household's questions. It is not offered to the public, and it is not a crawler.

Its traffic

Each search is a question someone in the household asked. The instance sends its queries one at a time, at most a few hundred a month to any one service, including Wikipedia and Wikidata.

Contact

To reach the owner about this instance's traffic, please open an issue on this repository.
